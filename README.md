## About

I am an undergraduate student at the School of Computer Science, Beijing University of Posts and
Telecommunications (BUPT). I expect to receive my B.S. degree in 2027 and to continue as a
direct-entry Ph.D. student in the same group, advised by Prof. Mengshi Qi.

My research is about making perception models for autonomous driving keep learning after
deployment: in particular continual learning and continual test-time adaptation for semantic
segmentation, and how multimodal large models can supervise and drive that adaptation over
long-tailed, continuously changing driving data. I am also interested in the evaluation
platforms needed to test such systems reproducibly.

## Research Interests

- Continual learning & continual test-time adaptation
- Semantic segmentation and scene understanding
- Autonomous driving perception
- Multimodal large language models / vision-language models
- Benchmarks and evaluation platforms
